Motif and motion

One wave field, fixed behind the whole page, drifting slowly. 34 lines with a cream → orange → ember gradient; back lines are thin, front lines thicker, which reads as depth instead of a flat pattern. The pause button stops it (moving content over 5 s needs a control). Reduced motion: still wave field. Reduced transparency: glass becomes solid.

Ember vs. a real second accent

  • Ember stays inside your palette's family, so the brand still reads as "orange". It fixes flatness by adding range (light → hot), not a new color.
  • Aqua is orange's opposite on the color wheel, so it makes the orange pop harder, and it's the classic aero color. Risk: past ~3% it dilutes your identity.

Still watch

  • Blur + animation cost battery on phones - we'll cap the field on small screens
  • "Too busy" risk: max one wave field per page, no extra decoration on cards
