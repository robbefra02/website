Motif and motion

One wave strip per page, in the header only. It sits nearly flat and swells when you point at or tab to a list entry - like a scope trace picking up a signal. No ambient motion, so no pause button needed. Reduced motion: static, flat waves.

Prioritizes

  • Fast scanning: date, title, tag in one line
  • Easiest to build and to post to
  • Calmest, the clearest fix for "too busy"

Trades off

  • Least visual impact; photos live inside posts, not on the list
  • The wave plays a small role
  • Mono text can feel "developer blog" if overused
